\chapter{Project Management Plan}

\section{Overview}

This chapter presents the project management framework governing the LivingDocs
capstone project, covering team organization, effort estimation, scheduling, risk
management, quality assurance, training, communication, and configuration management.
The project lifecycle spans six phases (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Testing,
Monitoring \& Controlling, and Closing), with a total estimated effort of
\textbf{232 man-days} distributed across a three-member team.

RSK-01 & GitHub API rate limit or Webhook notification failure preventing event ingestion. & High & Medium & Implement exponential backoff retries, event logging, and message queueing via Kafka/RabbitMQ. \\
\hline
RSK-02 & AI Model hallucination or inaccurate documentation suggestions generated by LLM. & High & Medium & Enforce strict Human-in-the-Loop review workflow (Staff \& Manager) and grounding evidence checks. \\
\hline
RSK-03 & Data security breach or exposure of sensitive GitHub OAuth tokens / API keys. & Critical & Low & Encrypt credentials at rest using Supabase secrets; enforce HTTPS/TLS and strict RBAC controls. \\
\hline
RSK-04 & Database vector synchronization latency between PostgreSQL and AI RAG engine. & Medium & Medium & Optimize HNSW vector indexes in Pgvector and implement asynchronous background indexing workers. \\
\hline
RSK-05 & Sprint schedule delays due to complex AST parsing across multiple programming languages. & High & Medium & Prioritize Java \& TypeScript first as core MVP targets; defer secondary languages to future releases. \\

\end{footnotesize}
